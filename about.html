Research interests

My interests include compound weather extremes, cloud and aerosol microphysics, lightning, regional climate modelling, energy meteorology, and machine learning for atmospheric and climate applications.

I work primarily with Python and Jupyter notebooks, combining atmospheric datasets with statistical analysis and scientific visualisation. My coursework also includes machine-learning applications such as classification and temperature downscaling.
